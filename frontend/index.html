Summary. CodeA4 scans a handwriting sample, learns its natural variation and drift, and renders uploaded source files onto print-ready A4 pages that look genuinely handwritten. No syntax colouring, no reformatting — whitespace and indentation are preserved exactly as written in the original file.

2. Your Style

Step 2 of 5

From your three sheets, we grouped each character into the small number of ways you naturally form it — plus a model of how your baseline and margin wander and self-correct.

2.1 Style sets

Each row is a fully internally-consistent alphabet, built from one cluster per character. Every stroke below is traced from your own sheets — not a stand-in font.

2.2 Drift model

Solid line: baseline drift, vertical, within a line. Dashed line: margin drift, horizontal, down the page.
